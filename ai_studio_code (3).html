Independent Leadership

Built on Intentional Craft & Clear Purpose

Vees Design represents Vivi's growing independent design practice. Conceived with an appreciation for balanced editorial aesthetics, the brand was established to deliver creative clarity without unnecessary studio bloat.

As the sole owner and creative director, Vivi personally guides each project from conceptual exploration to the finalized production files. Clients work directly with the creative decision-maker, ensuring that nuance, character, and client intentions are maintained across every revision.

Supported in outreach and collaborative networking by James, the studio remains lean, dedicated, and strictly focused on high quality visual delivery.
